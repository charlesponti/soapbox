Animated: Auto-Deprecation

Facts decay at different rates. When a class falls under the threshold, it gets archived.

Click "Simulate 180 days".

How it works

  1. Decay class is assigned at write time from the slot kind. Identity facts (name) are exempt; preferences fade at medium speed; passing observations fade fast.
  2. Effective confidence is a function of the stored confidence, reinforcement count, and elapsed time since last_reinforced_at.
  3. Reinforcement resets the clock: every time a memory is retrieved or restated, its decay restarts and confidence rises.
  4. Archival happens in the sleep job: anything below the threshold moves to archived, leaving history intact rather than deleting it.

The point is not to forget — it is to stop stale facts from outranking fresh ones at retrieval time.
